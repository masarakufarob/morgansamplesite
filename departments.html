Academic Departments

Morgan Zintec College is organised into four academic departments, each headed by a Head of Department and comprising multiple sections led by Lecturers-in-Charge.

📖

Department of Humanities

HOD: Mr B. Ziso

One of the largest departments at the college, comprising Languages, Social Sciences, and Contemporary subjects. Responsible for developing student teachers' soft skills, cultural competence and 21st century competencies.

Sections:

ChiShona IsiNdebele English FAREME Heritage-Social Studies Health & Life Skills NASS
