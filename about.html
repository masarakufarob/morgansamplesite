Principal's Desk

👤

Dr. T.J. Zendah

Principal

"The Morgan ZINTEC branded teacher should harness artificial intelligence as a pedagogic tool rather than be replaced by such. Education remains a socially interactive process, hence the perpetual significance of the teacher in the 21st century classroom."

Morgan Zintec College was brought into effect in 1981 as a rapid response to the acute shortage of teachers created by the democratization of education. The institution has been developing quality human capital in its General Course and Early Childhood Development (ECD) programmes, having adopted a conventional approach guided by the mission to produce primary school teachers with requisite skills for the community.

Morgan Zintec College draws from Zimbabwe's Vision 2030 — "towards a prosperous and empowered upper middle-income society by 2030." The College is informed by the value of responsiveness of Public Institutions, which expects that public servants deliver mandated services according to public demand. The public currently demands teachers with competencies, skills and attitudes to impart hard and soft skills that will transform life.

The institution in its bid to deliver lifelong education is informed by the Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education's doctrine on Education 5.0 premised on heritage, innovation and industrialization. A disruptive approach to training is taking root within the College, with more emphasis on integration of educational technology. It is no longer training as usual — the College is adopting adaptive, transformative and blended pedagogical strategies to produce innovative pre-service teachers for the 21st century and beyond.

The focus is on developing unique human intelligence making our product responsive to technological, social and economic fluidity in dispensing quality and appropriate education. Our core values — collaboration, innovation, patriotism, unhu/ubuntu, and equity — remain central to this vision.

Morgan Zintec College

"As the Morgan family we encourage cooperation, joint effort, solidarity, and fellowship as we are guided by equity — hence we are a college of excellence."

Morgan Zintec College strives to be a leading college of education in producing innovative and transformative educators for sustainable industrialization. Our core values of patriotism — a strong sense of belonging, identity, responsibility and accountability — guide all members of staff.

All members of staff are encouraged to have a sense of belonging and uphold the virtue of unhu/ubuntu where we expect everyone to be responsible, honest, just, trustworthy, considerate and cultured.

Morgan Zintec College welcomes, nurtures and trains all qualifying people into effective primary school teachers regardless of their gender, physical, intellectual, social, emotional, linguistic background or any other characteristics. The college understands the value of this diversity, and greater steps have been taken to ensure that all students' needs are catered for.

Our Story

College History

1981

College Established

Morgan Zintec College was established as a rapid response to the acute shortage of teachers following the democratization of education after independence. It initially shared premises with David Livingstone Primary School.

Early Years

Move to Current Campus — 1:10:1 Model

The college moved to its current location, taking over the boarding facilities of Morgan High School. The Zintec programme was offered as a four-year 1:10:1 model — one term at college, ten terms on teaching practice, one final term at college.

1995

Modified to 2-8-2 Model

The programme was revised to a 2-8-2 model: two terms at college, eight terms on teaching practice, and a final two terms at college.

2000

Modified to 2-5-2 Model (3-year programme)

In January 2000, the 2-8-2 programme was modified to a 2-5-2 model, reducing the course to three years. This programme was concluded in 2020.

2014

In-Service Programme Launched

The college launched the Diploma in Supervision and ICT for school-based mentors, initially in partnership with UNESCO. To date, 840+ mentors have graduated.

2019

Current 3-3-3 Model Adopted

In September 2019, the College adopted the 3-3-3 model — three terms at college, three terms on teaching practice, and three final terms at college — which all subsequent intakes follow.

Vision & Mission

Vision

A reputable college of education in the production of innovative and transformative educators for sustainable industrialization.

Mission

To produce skilled and enterprising 21st century educators using heritage-based philosophy for the attainment of inclusive and equitable quality education.

The College's motto is: "Education, Progress & Self Reliance"
