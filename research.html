Research Direction

About Our Research

Morgan Zintec College's research agenda is anchored in Education 5.0 — the Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education's doctrine on heritage, innovation and industrialization. The college is committed to evidence-based teacher education, generating knowledge that is responsive to Zimbabwe's national development goals and the 21st century global landscape.

The college encourages student teachers to put theories and perspectives into practice and to generate new, relevant theories grounded in heritage and indigenous knowledge systems.

Focus Areas

Research Areas

🎓

Teacher Education & Pedagogy

Innovative pedagogical approaches, blended learning, adaptive teaching strategies and 21st century competencies for pre-service teachers.

🌍

Heritage-Based Education

Integration of indigenous knowledge systems, cultural identity, Ubuntu/Unhu philosophy and heritage-based curriculum design.

💻

Educational Technology

ICT integration in teacher education, eLearning platforms, AI as a pedagogic tool, and digital literacy for educators.

🏛️

Education Governance

Corporate governance in educational institutions, accountability frameworks, stakeholder engagement and policy analysis.

🧒

Early Childhood Development

ECD policy, quality assurance in ECD centres, inclusive early childhood education and community-based ECD models.

🔬

Curriculum & Assessment

Curriculum development and reform, harmonised curriculum alignment, authentic assessment and learner-centred approaches.

By Department

Departmental Research Focus

📖 Humanities

Language education, indigenous knowledge systems, moral and values education, heritage-social studies and citizenship formation.

  • ChiShona and IsiNdebele pedagogy
  • Multilingual education in Zimbabwe
  • Values and character education
  • Health and life skills research

🔢 Maths & Sciences

Mathematics pedagogy, science methodology, STEM integration in primary education and inquiry-based learning approaches.

  • Mathematics anxiety and remediation
  • Science practical skills in primary schools
  • STEM curriculum integration

🔬 Research & Innovation

Educational innovation, action research, school-based research mentorship, and research methodology in teacher education.

  • Action research in teacher training
  • Mentor supervision studies
  • Innovation and industrialization

🧒 ECD Department

Early childhood policy, inclusive ECD, community-based programmes and developmental psychology in Zimbabwean contexts.

  • ECD centre quality standards
  • Play-based learning research
  • Inclusive early childhood education
