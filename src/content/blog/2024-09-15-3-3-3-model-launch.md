# Morgan Zintec College Launches New 3-3-3 Teacher Training Model

**Harare, September 15, 2024** — Morgan Zintec College is proud to announce the successful implementation of the new 3-3-3 teacher training model, adopted in September 2019, which replaces the previous 2-5-2 programme.

## A New Era in Teacher Education

The 3-3-3 model represents a significant evolution in how Morgan Zintec College prepares educators for Zimbabwe's classrooms. This innovative approach structures the three-year diploma programme into three distinct phases:

1. **Three Terms at College** — Intensive theoretical and pedagogical training
2. **Three Terms Teaching Practice** — Extended practical experience in schools
3. **Three Final Terms at College** — Advanced studies and professional consolidation

## Alignment with National Vision

Principal Dr. T.J. Zendah emphasised that this model directly supports Zimbabwe's Vision 2030 and the Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education's Education 5.0 framework, which prioritises heritage, innovation, and industrialisation.

> "Our 3-3-3 model ensures that student teachers receive balanced theoretical grounding and extensive practical experience, producing educators who are both knowledgeable and classroom-ready," said Dr. Zendah.

## Programme Benefits

- **Extended Teaching Practice**: Three full terms of supervised classroom experience
- **Blended Learning**: Integration of face-to-face and virtual learning approaches
- **Heritage-Based Curriculum**: Incorporation of indigenous knowledge systems and Ubuntu/Unhu philosophy
- **Technology Integration**: ICT skills development throughout the programme

## Graduation Outcomes

Since the adoption of the 3-3-3 model, the college has maintained its strong graduation rates:
- 840+ In-Service programme graduates since 2014
- 660+ ECD Short Course graduates
- Consistent employment rates for diploma graduates

## Looking Forward

The college continues to innovate, with plans to expand the Diploma in Applied ICT specialisations and strengthen research partnerships with local and international institutions.
