Who We Are

About Morgan Zintec College

Established in 1981 as a rapid response to Zimbabwe's teacher shortage, Morgan Zintec College has evolved through several training models — 1:10:1, 2-8-2, 2-5-2 — and now operates the current 3-3-3 Model adopted in September 2019.

Aligned with Zimbabwe's Vision 2030 and Education 5.0, the college integrates heritage, innovation and industrialization to produce 21st century educators equipped with both hard and soft skills.

Vision

A reputable college of education in the production of innovative and transformative educators for sustainable industrialization.

Mission

To produce skilled 21st century educators using heritage-based philosophy for inclusive and equitable quality education.

Programmes & Courses

Nationally accredited qualifications and specialist short courses preparing the next generation of Zimbabwean educators.

Junior Course

Diploma in Education (Primary) — Junior

Prepares student teachers for Junior level primary education (Grades 4–7) covering core subject areas, methodology and extended teaching practice.

  • Theory of Education & Professional Foundations
  • Subject Specialisation (one main subject)
  • Professional Studies A, B, C & D
  • 3 Terms Teaching Practice in primary schools
Learn More
Infant Course

Diploma in Education (Primary) — Infant

Prepares student teachers for Infant level (ECD–Grade 3) focusing on early literacy, numeracy and developmentally appropriate pedagogy.

  • Early Childhood Development Foundations
  • Infant Methodology & Subject Specialisation
  • Professional Studies A, B, C & D
  • 3 Terms Teaching Practice in infant schools

From the Vice Principal

A College of Excellence

"Morgan Zintec College strives to be a leading college of education in producing innovative and transformative educators for sustainable industrialization. Our core values of patriotism — a strong sense of belonging, identity, responsibility and accountability — guide everything we do."

The College welcomes, nurtures and trains all qualifying people into effective primary school teachers regardless of gender, physical, intellectual, social, emotional or linguistic background. As the Morgan family we encourage cooperation, joint effort, solidarity and fellowship — guided by equity.
